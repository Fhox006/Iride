Its bundled dependencies are gl-matrix 3.4.3 (MIT, gl-matrix-MIT.txt) and

Local fix: BlurRenderer.blur copies the input to its output when iteration is zero.
Upstream skips both loops in that case and leaves a black output. This permits crisp
lyrics while keeping the upstream raindrop simulation, textures, and refraction intact.

tv_lyrics/rain-static.webp is a still frame of the same upstream rain simulation
rendered against black. It is used when WebGL2 is unavailable.
